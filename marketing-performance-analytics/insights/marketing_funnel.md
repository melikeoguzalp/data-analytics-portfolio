# Marketing Funnel Analysis

## Objective

Analyze user progression through the marketing funnel at the session level.

## Query

sql/02_marketing_funnel.sql

## Business Insights

### Sessions

The analysis aggregates events at the session level instead of the event level, providing a more accurate representation of user journeys.

### Add to Cart

The number of sessions reaching the Add to Cart stage indicates purchase intent.

### Checkout

Comparing checkout sessions with cart sessions highlights potential friction points during checkout.

### Purchases

Purchase sessions are used to calculate the overall conversion rate.

### Marketing Performance

Sessions are grouped by:

- Source
- Medium
- Campaign

allowing comparison of marketing channel effectiveness.
